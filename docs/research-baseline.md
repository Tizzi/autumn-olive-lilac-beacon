# Pham vi hien tai

Oracle la pham vi duy nhat duoc duy tri trong repository nay.

## Quyet dinh

- Cac strategy cu da khong dat yeu cau ve hieu nang, drawdown hoac do ben va khong con la doi tuong nghien cuu.
- Khong co roadmap de refine, tune, audit, paper trade hoac live trade cac strategy cu trong repository nay.
- Ket qua cua cac strategy cu khong duoc dung lam bang chung cho mot edge, benchmark, hay dieu kien de phat trien Oracle.
- Oracle tap trung vao dashboard, backtest engine, du lieu, regime analysis va cac artifact da duoc luu trong chinh repository nay.

## Pham vi tai lieu

Tai lieu trong `docs/` chi phuc vu viec mo ta, tai lap va bao tri Oracle. `research-template.md` duoc giu nhu mot mau luu tru neu can ghi nhan mot thay doi hay ket qua cua Oracle trong tuong lai; no khong tao ra cam ket ve mot chuong trinh nghien cuu tiep theo.

## Luu y

Oracle la cong cu nghien cuu va quan sat. Ket qua hien tai khong la khuyen nghi dau tu, khong phai tin hieu giao dich, va khong phai mot he thong du dieu kien de live trading.
